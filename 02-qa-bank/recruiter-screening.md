# Q&A Bank — Recruiter / Screening

> Keep these 45–75 seconds. Pattern: situation → what I owned → measurable outcome → learning.

## Q: Tell me about yourself. (30-second version)

**Tags:** intro · **Asked at:** every screen

"I'm Abhav, a React Native developer with ~5 years across e-commerce, banking, and healthcare. At Best Buy I work on Search and PLP, and I built a Performance Debug Library from scratch — a zero-config in-app overlay tracking real-time FPS, memory, and TTID/TTFD, with uploads to Dynatrace and Sentry. That work fed into a 1.7-second first-click improvement and a 12% engagement lift. I focus on performance, clean architecture, and measurable impact."

## Q: What project are you most proud of, and why?

**Tags:** pride · **Asked at:** expected

"Tooling that makes the whole team better: the perf overlay gave engineers and QA a shared language for performance, reduced guesswork, and made optimization repeatable instead of hero-driven."

## Q: Why are you looking for a change?

**Tags:** motivation · **Asked at:** expected

"I've enjoyed hard performance and architecture problems at Best Buy; now I want that with more ownership and product depth. I'm drawn to environments that are fundamentals-first with high accountability for user-facing outcomes."

## Q: Why <company> specifically? (Zerodha version)

**Tags:** company-fit · **Asked at:** expected

"Three reasons: product impact at national scale; an engineering culture that values simplicity and craft over hype; and a role that expects end-to-end ownership including native and performance depth — exactly where I add value."

## Q: How do you define success in your first 90 days?

**Tags:** onboarding · **Asked at:** expected

"First 30: understand architecture, workflows, reliability baselines. Next 30: own one meaningful feature or optimization end-to-end. By 90: measurable impact shipped, plus something reusable left behind — tooling, docs, or quality guardrails."

## Q: What compensation are you expecting?

**Tags:** compensation · **Asked at:** expected

"A fair market package for a senior mobile engineer with architecture and performance ownership. I'm flexible based on scope, growth, and total structure." (Never give a number first if avoidable; if pushed, give a researched range.)

## Q: Do you have questions for us?

**Tags:** reverse · **Asked at:** always

- "Top two engineering outcomes this role should drive in the first six months?"
- "How do you measure app performance and prevent regressions before release today?"
- "Where do senior engineers spend most time here — shipping, architecture, or operational quality?"
