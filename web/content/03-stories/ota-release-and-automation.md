# Story: OTA Release in 4 Days (delivery under pressure)

> vs 1-week deadline · cross-timezone coordination

## STAR (30-second spoken version)

- **S** — Critical fix needed in production with a hard 1-week deadline; changes lived across branches with teams in multiple timezones.
- **T** — Get a safe OTA release out — fast, without breaking anything else.
- **A** — Scoped ruthlessly to the critical path, coordinated cherry-picks across timezones, drove release sign-offs in parallel instead of serially, verified with smoke suite + staged rollout.
- **R** — Shipped in 4 days — 3 days early. Release-team kudos.

## What it demonstrates

Delivery judgment: cutting scope, not corners. Parallel coordination. OTA/CodePush mechanics (what JS-only changes can ship OTA vs what needs a binary + store review).
